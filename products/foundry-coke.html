Foundry Coke

Hard, low-reactivity coke used as fuel and carbon source in cupola furnaces and foundry melting operations.

Overview

Consistent Melting Performance

Foundry coke is used where a strong, slow-burning carbon fuel is required. Its low reactivity and mechanical strength allow it to hold up under the load of a charged cupola while producing steady heat through the melt.

We supply foundry coke against the grade, size and volume each customer specifies, with the material confirmed before dispatch. Where a customer's process needs a particular size band or ash range, we source to that requirement rather than offering a fixed stock item.
