Carbon Dust

Fine carbon material supplied for recarburising, moulding and other industrial carbon applications.

Overview

Fine Carbon, Consistently Supplied

Carbon dust is a fine-grained carbon material used where carbon has to be added or distributed evenly rather than burned as lump fuel. It is commonly used in moulding mixtures and in adjusting the carbon content of a melt.

We supply carbon dust against a stated fineness and quantity, with material confirmed before dispatch and packing chosen to suit handling at your site. Because fine material needs careful handling, packing and loading are agreed in advance for each consignment.
