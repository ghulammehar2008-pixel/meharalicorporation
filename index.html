About Mehar Ali Corporation

A Reliable Partner for Industrial Supply

Mehar Ali Corporation is an industrial trading company based in Karachi, Pakistan, serving businesses that require dependable sourcing and supply of industrial materials. We focus on building reliable supplier relationships, maintaining quality standards, and delivering practical procurement solutions to our customers.

Our work covers sourcing from established suppliers, verifying material quality before dispatch, coordinating transport and delivery schedules, and staying available to customers throughout the process. We deal primarily in foundry coke, carbon dust and pet coke, and we treat every order as the beginning of a long-term working relationship rather than a single transaction.

  • Sourcing and supplier verification for industrial carbon materials
  • Delivery coordination across Karachi and other industrial regions
  • Direct, responsive communication from inquiry to dispatch
