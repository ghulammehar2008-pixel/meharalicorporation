Who we are

Industrial Trading, Done Properly

Mehar Ali Corporation is an industrial trading company based in Karachi, Pakistan, serving businesses that require dependable sourcing and supply of industrial materials. We focus on building reliable supplier relationships, maintaining quality standards, and delivering practical procurement solutions to our customers.

Our day-to-day work is straightforward: understand what a customer needs, locate material that genuinely matches that requirement, verify it before it moves, and coordinate the logistics so it arrives when it is needed. We deal in foundry coke, carbon dust and pet coke, and we keep our product range focused so that our knowledge of each material stays deep.

Being located in the Sindh Industrial Trading Estate places us close to the foundries, steel units and processing plants we serve, which makes coordination, inspection and delivery simpler for everyone involved.

  • Established supplier relationships for consistent material availability
  • Material verification before dispatch on every consignment
  • Transport and delivery coordination handled end to end
  • Direct communication with decision makers, not call queues
